## Registered semantics

Before diagnostic/research draws, source hashes, seeds and decisions were frozen in [registration](evidence/extended-coverage-v1-registration.json). Seeds: scores 2026101710, independent H0 scores 2026101711, independent fresh risk follow-up 2026101712. Each scenario has 10,000 streams; variants share potential streams, without tuning or seed search. Eight fixture tests passed before registration, including equality to the existing primary implementation, structural ties, no resurrection, tolerance boundary, pending review, injected G1 failure, fixed risk targeting and cost/coverage accounting.

The pooled e-process is unchanged: alpha .05, bet .3, E<=.05 futility, at least three net wins, all nine strata observed, primary cap 100. First acceptance is latched. Only then does balanced nine-stratum sampling continue, counting primary items toward coverage, to a total main-study cap of 400. The extension does not generate new pooled acceptances or retest E. A first G1 safety deficit rejects. At completed coverage, G2 is evaluated once on all cumulative per-stratum paired counts: net losses greater than t reject; exactly t passes. No early G2 veto. Incomplete coverage is insufficient_power_coverage, never retain_incumbent.

Literal coverage requires every one of nine strata to reach m discordances. The diagnostic exempts the three structural-tie safety strata only from discordance coverage; they remain subject to G1 and G2. This is a labelled alternative, not an undisclosed change or an eligible replacement for the requested design.

G1 remains the zero-misfire fixture from the recorded v3/v4 dev/holdout evidence in doc 118. All three safety strata are both-correct ties; the six answer strata have .375 discordance probability, preserving 75% overall ties. G1 false-veto is zero in every row, conditional on this empirical fixture, not a population zero-risk guarantee.

The bad answer stratum rotates across six at p=.30; other answer strata are .91875 (requested fixture), giving overall discordant p=.815625 because safety strata have no discordances. A pre-registered .96-other-strata sensitivity restores overall p=.85. False veto means actual G1/G2 rejection among all good proposals; insufficient coverage is separate. Catch means an actual G2 rejection identifying the deliberately bad stratum among all regressing proposals, consistent with docs 117–118. Total nonpromotion and conditional catch are separate; neither silently replaces this target. H0 is per-study, not across-study FWER.

## Unconditional risk-stratum N=40

The risk stratum is prospectively fixed as supported-measurement. Every primary-accepted candidate receives 40 fresh independent items there, regardless of observed net losses or whether G2 has already rejected it. This gives a main-plus-follow-up maximum of 440 items. Net loss rejects; nonnegative net with at least one discordance clears; zero discordances remains review. It cannot resurrect a failed/incomplete main study. The fixed risk stratum matches the rotating regressed stratum in 1,667/10,000 proposals; the aligned subset is reported explicitly and is not chosen after observing outcomes.

## Bank v4 capacity and generation/sealing cost

A CPU-only capacity benchmark generated and independently truth-validated **900 dev + 1,800 reserve items (200 per stratum)** using the existing family-B templates, wrote and hashed a manifest, and sealed the nine reserve files to mode 000. Measured generation/validation/write: 1.0798 seconds; manifest/sealing: 0.0008 seconds; total **1.0806 seconds**, serialized items 6,844,835 bytes. **Zero model calls, zero GPU hours, $0 incremental cloud compute.** Local electricity is not priced.

This is a public-seed benchmark, NOT an eligible bank-v4 holdout, and is labelled unusable for study evidence. No actual bank v4 was commissioned or canonical manifest changed. Production generation must register a fresh private seed/version and split/consumption protocol, validate disjointness, seal before candidate design, and maintain gate-private content. The current v3 builder hardcodes 300 holdout items and three slices; a larger reserve needs a versioned reserve builder/allocator, not reuse of that slice manifest. This estimate covers generation/sealing at existing template complexity, not new adversarial-template design or clinician annotation. [Benchmark receipt](evidence/bank-v4-capacity-benchmark-receipt.json).

With 200 reserve items per stratum, a balanced 400-item main study consumes at most 45 per stratum, leaving at least 155. One unconditional 40-item risk follow-up fits, leaving at least 115 on its risk stratum. With the same registered risk stratum on every study, two worst-case 400+40 studies fit there (170 of 200); a third is not guaranteed. Without the risk follow-up, four worst-case balanced 400-item studies fit per-stratum capacity. Smaller actual stops may permit more; a real ledger must allocate fresh items atomically.
