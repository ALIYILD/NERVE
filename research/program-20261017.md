## Loop 47.2 — completion faithfulness

Adopted: a black-box, fail-closed action-report consistency gate, inspired by
[MCAIR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19679). It compares each Hermes agent's own
structured self-report with a host-owned journal, retaining source, test, tool
and fallback claims and their multiplicities. Unknown or missing reports and
incomplete journals are mismatches. This checks reported actions, not private
reasoning or semantic truth. The current no-tool Hermes adapter journals the
supplied context and its disabled tool/test capabilities; provider-internal
behavior is outside this observation boundary. Agent-node remains unadmitted
until its adapter supplies equally complete journals and self-reports.

Any mismatch yields `unfaithful_report`: quarantine before evaluation,
keep/revert, experience ingestion, calibration feedback or W1 handoff. The
rejection memory records seam + failure cluster, reason and evidence hash.
Every trace/report/label triple is immutable content-addressed evidence under
`docs/galahad/evidence/faithfulness/`. Audit evidence may be stored before a
scientific verdict; it is not a learned lesson or trusted experience. The
hypotheses remain proposals only. Structured reports are required; unsupported
free prose fails closed, rather than being guessed into an empty claim set.

Deferred: **47.2 white-box probe**. No activations are collected and no probe is
trained. The extractor's bounded `source_id` enum is the structural equivalent
of restricting source attribution to permitted evidence; it is not an internal
representation probe and does not establish completion faithfulness.

D10: faithfulness monitor ran on every Socratic and agent-node experiment, with
eligible, checked, missing, quarantined and per-kind counts. Both Hermes agents
are checked even when the chosen hypothesis is template-generated. Zero live
eligible experiments is reported as unexercised, not as demonstrated coverage.
Evidence: leaderboard `report_trace_consistency`, status coverage, doc 13x and
the trace/report/label seed directory. Fixture evidence is explicitly labelled
and excluded from experiment counts.
